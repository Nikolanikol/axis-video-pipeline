> **Updated 18 November 2026.** Rates and rules change, and public sources do not always agree. This
> article is a guide, not a calculation: confirm the figures with **Kosovo Customs** or **Albanian
> Customs**, or with your customs broker, before buying.

## The route: from Korea to new plates

1. **Choose.** Find the car on Encar or another Korean site — how to read a listing: [Encar in English](/en/encar/).
2. **Check.** Insurance history, inspection sheet, mileage and VIN — before paying.
3. **Buy and export.** The Korean exporter buys the car, deregisters it for export and prepares the papers.
4. **Ship.** By sea — ro-ro or container — to the port of Durrës (for Kosovo often via Thessaloniki too).
5. **Clear customs.** Duty, excise (in Kosovo) and VAT are paid.
6. **Register.** Technical inspection and plates.

## Papers from Korea

- **Export deregistration certificate** — 수출말소증명서. Without it the car cannot legally leave Korea.
- **Invoice** — the car value that VAT is based on.
- **Bill of lading** — the sea transport document.
- **Technical documents** and, if required, proof of the Euro standard.

## What you pay in Kosovo

| Charge | Rate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Customs duty | 10% | General rate; Korea is not in CEFTA |
| Excise | by engine and age | Diesel pays more than petrol |
| VAT | 18% | On the customs value plus duty and excise |

**Age limit:** under a 2017 law, imported vehicles cannot be older than **10 years**, and at least
**Euro 4** is often required. Check the year before buying — an 11-year-old car may not clear customs at all.

## What you pay in Albania

| Charge | Rate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 20% | On the customs value — sources agree |
| Customs duty | 0–10% depending on the source | Sources disagree — confirm with Albanian Customs |
| Excise and registration fees | vary | Check before buying |

For Albania, public sources disagree on duty, excise and the age limit. Ask your customs broker for the
final figure in writing before paying.

## What to check before paying

- The car year — especially for **Kosovo** (10-year limit).
- The engine’s **Euro** standard.
- Who pays shipping and insurance to the port.
- The clearance cost — in writing from the customs broker.
- The car history: accidents, mileage, VIN — see the glossary in [Encar in English](/en/encar/).
